# v0.9 vs v0.12 Comparison Against Empirical Observables

This note exists to force the model to answer to the papers rather than to my own enthusiasm.

The relevant empirical targets are now clear enough.

Karmin 2015 gives the skyline target:
- a second strong bottleneck in Y lineages in the last 10 ky
- a dip around roughly 8–4 kya
- mtDNA continuing upward while Y drops
- female effective size up to 17-fold higher than male during the dip. 

Poznik 2016 gives the punctuated-burst target:
- bursts of extreme male-lineage expansion in the last 15 ky
- these occur independently across all five continental superpopulations
- rapid nodes are interpreted with a two-phase growth model and minimum sons-per-generation estimates. 

Wang 2013 gives the strongest regional starburst target:
- three star-like expansions
- dated approximately 5.4 kya, 6.5 kya, and 6.8 kya
- together accounting for more than 40% of present Han Chinese patrilines. 

Zeng 2018 gives the nearest rival structured-mechanism benchmark:
- bottleneck around 5000–7000 BP
- female line comparatively stable because of exogamy
- extinction of many Y clades under patrilineal group competition
- structured demes rather than one universal bowl.

## Current reading

v0.9 remains the best single-basin behavioral family.

Why:
- bounded compression remains strong
- survivor concentration remains strongest
- damage behaves as a real interior state variable
- timing is better than the cleaner recovery-channel variants

v0.12 is the best architectural advance.

Why:
- global bounded-compression shape improves materially
- pre-window loss is lower
- aggregate lag improves
- survivor monopoly weakens
- structured-basin logic is represented honestly for the first time

But v0.12 is not yet the winner overall.

Why not:
- local basin pruning still occurs too early relative to local windows
- rebound remains substantial
- the aggregate improvement appears to come partly from superposition rather than from well-timed local transitions

## Current blunt conclusions

1. The model is no longer allowed to claim merely that “culture matters.”
   It now supports the narrower claim that bounded tightening of coupled filters can produce strong male-line compression without requiring singular catastrophe.

2. Informal reproduction is not inherently egalitarian.
   If it tracks formal success, it amplifies hierarchy; if it is more independent, it preserves breadth better.

3. Hysteresis matters, but where it enters matters more.
   A cleaner recovery-channel story was too weak. The best current single-basin result requires persistence itself to become stickier.

4. Single-basin mean field was a major topological cheat.
   v0.12 shows that some global shape is more naturally produced in an archipelago than in one universal bowl.

5. The project now has two honest frontiers:
   - add explicit female-channel comparison
   - make Daisyworld local timing as credible as its aggregate shape

## Present best statement

The project currently supports the following statement:

A post-Neolithic Y bottleneck can be modeled as bounded tightening of coupled reproductive filters, with informal channels that may amplify rather than dissolve hierarchy, with partial hysteresis whose behavioral force depends on where memory enters the transfer function, and with aggregate global shape improved by representing the world as several partially closed social basins rather than one universal field.

What is still missing is equally clear:
- explicit male/female asymmetry in the model,
- durable post-window lock-in,
- and cleanly staggered local basin transitions.
